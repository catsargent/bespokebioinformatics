Why Bespoke Bioinformatics?

Considerable time, effort and money are spent when conducting laboratory experiments. Custom bioinformatics analysis allows you to get the most out of your data, to leverage unique insights and ensure that your research advances.

  1. Initial consultation

    In a free initial meeting we discuss the project details and expected outcomes. Following this meeting, time and cost estimates will be provided.

  2. Bioinformatics analysis

    After project terms have been agreed, the client provides access to the data and bioinformatics analysis begins.

  3. Presenting results

    Regular updates are provided to the client (via video calls, emails and/or messaging apps). These discussions often feed back into the bioinformatics analysis, making this step and the prior one an iterative process.

  4. Project completion

    Upon completion of the project (as defined by prior-agreed milestones), all data and scripts are shared with the client alongside any other agreed documents e.g. reports, figures, methods write-up etc.
